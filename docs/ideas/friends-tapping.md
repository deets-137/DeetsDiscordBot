# Friends tapping their own apps

## 1. What he said (2026-11-10)
"Can other people use the bot to tap their own apps if they have spotify for example?"
Accepted the recommendation not to build it.

## 2. Why not
- The tap runs on this PC; it hears only this PC's apps. A friend's app would need a sender
  program on their machine streaming to this bot over the internet: an open path into this PC,
  per-friend auth, and code on their machines.
- A bot has one voice connection per server, so only one source plays at a time anyway.
- Spotify's and Apple Music's terms restrict rebroadcasting to a group.

## 3. What friends use instead
Discord's **Go Live** shares a window with its sound; **Spotify Listen Along** lets friends
listen along through Discord.
