## 1. Learned 2026-11-10
- **Measure before you blame.** Silence in the call had three different causes in one sitting:
  DeetsMusic at 17% in the Windows mixer, the wrong process captured, and davey 0.2.0. Each
  was found by a measurement (`--meters`, a peak level, `/vc tone` with the DAVE readout), not
  by a guess. Lead with the measurement.
- **Neat lists matter.** He noticed that a suggestion list's column did not line up, in the
  sample, before he saw it in Discord. A list's columns are the same width for every row.
- **Quiet over chatty.** A card that edits itself every 15 s was too much even though it was
  under every limit. Edit on a change.
- **He wants the people in the call to feel part of it**: what plays, where their song landed
  and when it plays, the channel's status line. Prefer what everyone sees over what only the
  host sees.
- **Use what DeetsMusic already decided.** Permissions came from its Rooms rules, the accent
  from its theme. Look there first.
- **Keep his running things running.** He was listening while the work went on: build beside
  the live helper, swap files the running process doesn't hold, and ask him to restart.
